How the formula works

For a known percentile, the central view uses 1 + (100 − percentile) ÷ 100 × (candidate count − 1) to compute the rank. This is the standard AIEEE / JEE Main formula. For marks, a marks-to-percentile interpolation is applied based on historical anchor points.
